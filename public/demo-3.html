Scroll down to see the 3 video behaviors

We are using a sample Big Buck Bunny video for the demo.

Behavior 1

Always Autoplay

The video loops continuously in the background, providing constant motion. Simple and visually rich.

Behavior 2

Play on Hover

The video is paused and dimmed until the user hovers their mouse over the card, creating an interactive reveal.

Behavior 3

Play when Centered

The video pauses when off-screen and only plays when the card enters the center of the viewport during the scroll.
